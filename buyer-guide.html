THE NOV PETS BUYER GUIDE

Choose with
a clearer picture.

A short guide to the details worth comparing when you’re building a dog travel range.

01 / BUYER CHECK

Start with the space.

Seat covers protect the rear bench; boot liners cover the cargo area. Compare coverage, attachment points and access. Measure dimensions in millimetres, rather than relying on a universal-fit description.

02 / BUYER CHECK

See the construction.

Review fabric, backing, seams and fasteners together. Ask which model and components any waterproof or cleaning claims apply to. A cover or liner protects the vehicle interior; it does not restrain the dog.

03 / BUYER CHECK

Plan the collection.

For coordinated sets, compare components, size ranges, colours and logo methods. Agree how mixed sizes and colours affect minimum quantities and how each combination will be packed.

04 / BUYER CHECK

Compare the packed product.

Include unit packaging, weight, units per carton and carton dimensions in your comparison. They affect storage, fulfilment and landed cost.

05 / BUYER CHECK

Approve a clear standard.

Keep the sample reference, specification, artwork, packaging and inspection criteria together. Record any changes before production.

06 / BUYER CHECK

Compare quotes consistently.

Use the same currency, quantity, delivery basis and destination. Separate production from transit time and clarify freight, duties, GST and importer responsibilities.
