ANSWERS FOR BUSINESS BUYERS

Bowl and water dispenser FAQS

Does the water dispenser need power or certification as an electrical product?+

No. It works by gravity with no pump, cable or battery, so it is not an electrical product. Material and labelling claims still need to match the selected model.

Which capacity should I choose?+

The 3.8 L and 5.7 L sizes suit single pets and smaller homes; 7.2 L and 8.6 L suit large dogs or multi-pet homes. You can mix capacities in one enquiry; minimum quantities are confirmed per capacity.

Plastic or stainless-steel bowls?+

Plastic bowls keep the retail price lower and come in two colours; stainless-steel bowls are more durable and easier to sell as a premium option. Many ranges offer both.

Can I put my own logo on the bowls or dispenser?+

Logo printing and branded packaging can be discussed for both products. The method, position and minimum run are confirmed with the selected model, size and colour.
