A range for business buyers

Our enquiries are for wholesale purchasing and private label projects. Retailers can start with a category and approximate quantity; brands can add a logo or packaging idea. Importers can include their destination and packing requirements. You do not need a finished specification to start a conversation.

Seat covers and boot liners protect different vehicle areas. A coordinated accessory set is a separate assortment of walking and travel pieces. Choose the role of each item before deciding which variants belong in your collection.

Compare models before comparing prices

Dimensions, construction, colour combinations and packing affect the quotation. Approve the selected sample and keep a written specification. Product minimums, logo-label runs and packaging quantities may differ; review them separately.

Images on this site illustrate product directions. Final model availability, materials, MOQ, sample charges and timing are confirmed in the quotation. No local Australian or New Zealand stock is implied.

ANSWERS FOR PROCUREMENT TEAMS

Product range FAQS

Can I enquire about more than one product category?+

Yes. Select the relevant categories in the quote form and describe your intended range. Quantities and commercial terms are reviewed by product and variant; combining categories does not establish a shared minimum order quantity.

Are boot liners and cargo liners separate ranges?+

They refer to protection for the vehicle load area. Compare the actual floor, seat-back, side-wall and bumper coverage on the boot liner page, rather than choosing by the name alone.

What is included in a private label dog travel set?+

Harness, lead and collar combinations are starting points, with optional walking and travel accessories. Use the set planner to choose components, then confirm sizes, colours, logos and packing during sampling.

Where is NOVpets based?+

NOVpets is based in Shanghai, China. We support B2B sourcing enquiries from Australian and New Zealand pet businesses. Delivery terms, freight and destination requirements are reviewed for the order; we do not claim local warehouse stock.

How do I request wholesale prices and sample details?+

Send your selected range, approximate quantity and destination through Get a Free Quote. If you have a reference image, dimensions or branding idea, mention it in Message. Model-specific prices, minimum quantities and sample terms are confirmed after the product discussion.
