WHOLESALE BUYER NOTES

Compare standard, branded and custom orders.

Wholesale dog travel products are quoted against an agreed product and order mix. A headline MOQ or a single unit price is not enough to compare different suppliers.

Order routeStarting specificationWhat needs separate confirmation

Minimum quantities and sample costs

MOQ, sample availability and preparation time depend on the actual product, colour, size and customisation. Request written confirmation for your proposed variant mix. Sample charges, branding setup and shipping should be itemised separately from the bulk order.

Approve a reference sample and specification before production. If an artwork, material or component changes after approval, record the change and agree whether another sample is required.

Shipping and a comparable quotation

Record the currency, quantity, delivery term and named place with every price. Ask whether freight, insurance, duties, import GST and local clearance are included. Do not compare an ex-works unit price with a delivered price as though they cover the same costs.

Separate sample preparation, production, dispatch and transit timing. For Australian or New Zealand destinations, use the Australia sourcing checklist or New Zealand sourcing checklist to plan packaging, documentation and importer responsibilities.

NOV PETS focuses on B2B product sourcing. Local Australian or New Zealand stock, fixed delivery dates, test certification and product-specific performance are not implied; these details belong in the agreed quotation and specification.

PRIVATE LABEL ORDER PLANNING

Plan product, label and packaging quantities separately.

A private label dog travel order can involve several minimum quantities: the product variant, the logo application, a woven-label run and a printed-packaging run. Ask for each quantity separately so you can compare the complete order.

What each minimum quantity applies to
QuantityWhat to confirmWhat to keep in the quotation
Product or componentMinimum quantity per model, size and colour. For sets, check each component rather than only the total set count.Model reference, dimensions, variant quantities and agreed construction.
Logo application or label runApplication setup, label production quantity and whether the same approved label can be used across different products.Artwork version, unit or setup charge, labels used now and any paid balance. Agree ownership, storage and reuse terms if labels remain.
Retail packagingMinimum print run for each box, hangtag or insert design, including variants with different barcodes or contents lists.Packaging artwork, quantity supplied, packing method and any unused packaging balance.

A worked example: 240 sets, six variants

For planning only, suppose a range contains three harness sizes and two colours, with an equal quantity of each combination. That makes six set variants: 240 sets ÷ 6 = 40 sets per size-and-colour combination.

If every set contains one harness and one same-length lead, the plan needs 40 harnesses per size-and-colour variant and 120 leads per colour. Component minimums still need checking; a 240-set total does not establish that every variant meets its minimum quantity.

Keep leftover labels visible

If, in a separate hypothetical example, a supplier quotes a run of 1,000 labels and the first order uses 240 labels, 760 labels remain. Confirm who pays for that balance, where it is kept, whether it can be reused and how long storage is available.

These numbers illustrate purchasing calculations only. They are not NOVpets MOQ, stock, sample or label-storage commitments. The available quantities and terms for your selected products require a written quotation.

BEFORE YOUR FIRST ORDER

Your wholesale
questions.

What is your minimum order quantity?+

MOQ depends on the product, colour and level of customisation. Tell us which products you’re considering and an approximate quantity, if you have one. We can start by discussing standard products or a branded range.

Can I add my logo and customise the packaging?+

Let’s plan your logo, labels, hangtags and packaging together. Share your preferred finish or a reference image; the available methods and order requirements will be matched to the selected product.

Can I choose what goes into a travel set?+

Yes — use the set planner to select your preferred combination of walking and travel accessories. You can also request a different combination in your enquiry. Final components, colours and size options are agreed before sampling.

Can I see a sample before placing an order?+

Start with a sample discussion so you can review the product, fit, materials and branding. Sample costs, preparation time and shipping are confirmed for your selected products.

Can I mix sizes or colours?+

Include your preferred size and colour mix in your enquiry. We’ll review the combination and confirm minimum quantities for each option with the quotation.

What about lead times and delivery to Australia?+

Your quotation should separate sample preparation, production and shipping time. We’ll confirm the dispatch location, delivery destination and quotation terms for your order. Australian or New Zealand stock is not implied.
