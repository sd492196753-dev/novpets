WHOLESALE BUYER NOTES

Build a private label set with a clear component list.

A custom dog travel set is a coordinated assortment of walking and travel accessories. Buyers choose the components and branding direction; the final specification and availability are confirmed before an order.

Specification checklist for coordinated dog accessory sets
ComponentWhat to agreePacking decision
HarnessSize chart, adjustment range, construction and logo position. Walking use is separate from vehicle-restraint use.List quantity per size and whether each harness is packed separately.
Lead and collarLength or adjustment range, webbing, hardware and coordinated colours.Define set combinations and label each variant clearly.
Waste-bag holderMaterial, opening and attachment details.Confirm whether bags are included or the holder is supplied empty.
Travel bowl or bottleCapacity, materials, cleaning instructions and intended use.Check material information and any destination-market requirements for the selected component.
Retail packagingLogo, artwork, contents list, barcode and care or usage information.Specify one coordinated retail pack or individually packaged items.

Start with branding or change the product?

Adding a label to an existing product and developing a new size or material are different projects. Separate the component specification from logo methods and packaging artwork. Each can affect minimum quantities, sampling and production timing.

Use the planner above to send your preferred combination. A reference colour or image helps start the conversation, but approve physical samples and artwork before treating a coordinated colour match as agreed.

Keep a size and colour matrix

For a harness, collar and lead set, specify which sizes belong together and how many sets you need per combination. A total order quantity alone can hide very small quantities in individual variants.

Agree how replacements, mixed sizes and incomplete sets will be labelled and packed. Compare the full set’s landed cost with the price and packaging of each component. See wholesale samples and quotation terms.

ANSWERS FOR BUSINESS BUYERS

Custom set sourcing FAQS

Can I choose the components in a private label dog travel set?+

Yes, you can use the planner or describe your preferred mix in the enquiry. Harnesses, leads, collars and optional travel accessories are starting points for discussion. Final component availability, dimensions, colours and quantities are confirmed during specification and sampling.

Can I order different harness sizes in one set range?+

Include the quantity per size and colour, plus the matching collar and lead combination. Minimum quantities are checked at the component and variant level; a mixed range is not automatically covered by one total MOQ.

Do I need finished artwork before asking for a quote?+

No. Start with your preferred components, approximate quantity and a branding idea. Artwork files, application method and packaging details can be agreed after the initial product discussion.

Are the harnesses in a travel set vehicle safety harnesses?+

The illustrated harnesses and walking accessories are not presented as vehicle restraints or crash-tested equipment. If a vehicle-restraint product is required, it needs a separate specification and supporting evidence.
