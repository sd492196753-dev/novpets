WHOLESALE BUYER NOTES

1. Specify the order by product, size and colour.

Seat covers and boot liners

For rear-seat bench or hammock covers, record rear-seat dimensions, headrest fixings, seat-belt openings and side coverage. For boot or cargo liners, record load-floor depth, width between wheel arches, seat-back height and any side walls or bumper flap. “SUV”, “wagon” and “ute” describe different vehicle contexts; confirm the actual installation and do not assume a boot liner is suitable for an open ute tray.

Use the sample to review seams, backing, fixings and the stated cleaning method. Request evidence for any waterproof, washable or vehicle-fit claim for the selected model.

Coordinated private label sets

Plan the harness, lead, collar and optional travel accessories in a custom dog travel set. Specify the component list, size measurement points and quantities per colour and size. Ask whether different components have separate minimum quantities before finalising a set price.

Use one approved logo file and agreed colour reference across woven labels, hangtags and retail packaging. Agree the set assembly, barcode position and retail pack rather than assuming every component is packed individually.

WHOLESALE BUYER NOTES

2. Compare the complete New Zealand landed cost.

Request one consistent quantity, currency, Incoterm and named place from each supplier. An overseas product price and a New Zealand delivered price cover different costs.

WHOLESALE BUYER NOTES

3. Check NZ Customs and biosecurity separately.

New Zealand Customs

The New Zealand Customs import guidance covers documentation, clearances, import declarations, valuation and other import requirements. Ask your broker which steps apply to your specific shipment and importer.

Confirm the tariff classification, Customs value, duty and GST treatment against the actual goods. Customs notes that preferential tariff duty rates may apply to qualifying origins; do not assume eligibility from a supplier's country alone. Ask what origin evidence is needed.

Biosecurity enquiries

Use the Ministry for Primary Industries import portal or contact MPI and your broker to check product-specific biosecurity requirements. Provide the material composition, whether goods are new or used, origin, packaging and any timber-pallet details.

A category label such as “dog travel accessories” is not a clearance decision. Keep the invoice and packing list consistent with the approved product and packed shipment. Confirm any required documents before dispatch.

This is a buying checklist, not a tariff ruling, tax calculation or product compliance certificate. It does not certify that a particular shipment meets New Zealand import requirements. Check current official guidance for the actual goods.

WHOLESALE BUYER NOTES

4. Send a short buying brief.

  1. Range and intended use

    Tell us whether you want seat covers, boot liners or branded accessory sets. Include dimensions or reference images if available.

  2. Quantity and branding

    Share your approximate order size and proposed colour/size mix. Note whether you want a logo, retail packaging or changes to materials and construction.

  3. NZ destination and timing

    Add your delivery city or postcode and desired delivery window. Request separate sampling, production and freight estimates.

  4. Confirm before ordering

    Request model-specific MOQ, sample charges, packed dimensions, dispatch location, delivery terms and an approved specification in writing.

ANSWERS FOR NZ BUYERS

New Zealand sourcing FAQS

Is NOVpets a New Zealand local-stock wholesaler?+

NOVpets is based in Shanghai, China. This website offers B2B sourcing discussions for NZ buyers; it does not advertise a New Zealand warehouse or guaranteed local stock. Confirm dispatch location and delivery terms for the selected order.

Can I enquire about a private label range for New Zealand?+

Yes. Enquire about seat covers, boot liners or coordinated accessory sets with your proposed product mix, quantity and branding. Available methods, samples and minimum quantities are confirmed for the actual products.

Are MOQ and sample charges fixed for every product?+

No. Request confirmation for each model, colour, size and customisation method. Keep sample preparation, branding setup and shipping separate from the bulk order quotation.

Are NZ duty and GST included in the quotation?+

Confirm the currency, Incoterm, named place and included costs in writing. Do not assume product price or freight includes import duty, GST, clearance or local delivery.
