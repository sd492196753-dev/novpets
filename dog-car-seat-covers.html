WHOLESALE BUYER NOTES

Bench or hammock: what should your range include?

Dog car seat covers protect the rear-seat upholstery. For a wholesale range, compare the coverage layout, vehicle measurements and installation access before comparing prices.

Rear-seat cover options for wholesale sourcing
FormatCoverage to compareSample checks
Bench coverRear-seat base and backrest; check whether side flaps are included.Measure panel width and depth in mm. Check headrest straps, seat-belt openings and passenger access.
Hammock coverRear-seat protection with a panel extending towards the front-seat headrests.Check strap adjustment, footwell coverage and whether the panel can be folded away.
Side-flap optionAdditional coverage beside the rear seat; shape and depth depend on the model.Check door clearance, zips and how the flap is secured during use.

Specify the complete construction

Record the outer fabric, backing, padding, seams and fasteners separately. A fabric label such as “Oxford” or a denier number does not establish the performance of the finished cover. Ask for the material composition and a sample of the proposed construction.

Water resistance, washing instructions and anti-slip performance need evidence for the agreed model. Openings and seams may behave differently from the main panel. Review the underside on the intended upholstery and check for marking or colour transfer.

Make each SKU ready for resale

Confirm folded dimensions, packed weight, units per carton and carton size. Record logo placement, installation instructions, care labels and barcode positions together with the product specification.

Ask for MOQ by model, colour and branding method. Standard covers, logo applications and new construction may have different order requirements. Compare wholesale terms before approving a production order.

A seat cover is an interior protector, not a vehicle restraint or a crash-tested safety device. Walking accessories and attachment straps should not be described as crash protection.

ANSWERS FOR BUSINESS BUYERS

Seat cover sourcing FAQS

Can I source private label dog car seat covers?+

NOV PETS handles enquiries for logo, label and packaging options on dog car seat covers. Share the layout, quantity and branding idea; the available method and minimum quantity are confirmed for the selected product before sampling.

Does a universal size fit every Australian vehicle?+

No single measurement establishes fit for every vehicle. Compare rear-seat width and depth, headrest configuration, seat-belt access and side-flap clearance. Record the sample dimensions and intended vehicle examples rather than promising universal fit.

What should I include in a wholesale seat cover enquiry?+

A bench or hammock preference, approximate quantity and delivery destination are enough to start. Add dimensions, colour, logo and packaging requirements if known. You do not need a complete specification to submit the first enquiry.

How can I compare waterproof seat cover claims?+

Ask which layers and seams the claim covers and what test conditions were used. Review a finished sample and agreed care instructions. A water-resistant surface alone does not demonstrate that the entire assembled cover is waterproof.
