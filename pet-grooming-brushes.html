ANSWERS FOR BUSINESS BUYERS

Grooming brush sourcing FAQS

Are these brushes suitable for both cats and dogs?+

Yes. Both models are slicker brushes for removing loose undercoat on cats and dogs. The T9 is compact; the Max and Pro versions suit larger or longer coats. Tell us your target customer and we will recommend a model mix.

Can I put my own logo on the brushes?+

Logo printing on the handle and branded retail packaging can be discussed for both models. The available method, minimum run and artwork position are confirmed with the selected model and colour.

Can I mix models and colours in one order?+

Yes. List the model, version and colour quantities in your enquiry. Minimum quantities are confirmed per model and colour, so a combined order total does not establish every variant’s minimum.

Do you hold stock in Australia or New Zealand?+

No. NOVpets is based in Shanghai, China. Dispatch location, freight and delivery terms are agreed for each order; no local stock is implied.
